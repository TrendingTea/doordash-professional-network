# DoorDash Professional Network - Executive Proposal

## Author perspective
Authored by an active Gold Status Dasher with 4+ years of first-hand experience with the Dasher app, restaurant pickup workflows, delivery operations, and customer service. The author has a genuine service-provider relationship with DoorDash through the Dasher platform. This remains an independent product proposal and does not imply DoorDash corporate sponsorship, endorsement, approval, or employment.

## Executive thesis
Extend the delivery network into a professional-development and economic-mobility layer. Structured, observable merchant feedback can become optional professional evidence while Dashers control profiles containing skills, education, career interests, and recognition metrics.

## Market case
The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reports approximately 1.53 million delivery truck driver and driver/sales-worker jobs in 2024 and approximately 171,400 projected annual openings over 2024-2034, with 8% projected employment growth.

Pew Research Center found that 94% of U.S. adults viewed gig-platform work as a good way to earn extra money and 94% as a good way to work flexibly, but only 31% viewed it as a good way to build a career.

## Product layers
1. Merchant Quality Loop
2. Dasher Professional Profile
3. Customer-facing professional card
4. Trust and aggregation layer
5. Career Opportunity Layer
6. Pilot analytics

## Hypothetical McDonald's pilot
Use 50 participating locations over an eight-week period with structured post-pickup feedback and voluntary professional profiles. McDonald's is a hypothetical case study only; no endorsement or participation is implied.
